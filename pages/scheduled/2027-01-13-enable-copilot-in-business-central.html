How to Turn On Copilot in Business Central: Admin Steps and What to Check First

Quick answer

Copilot is built into Business Central online and cannot be switched off as a whole. Administrators control it per capability. Search for "Copilot & agent capabilities", review the Allow data movement setting if it appears, and activate or deactivate each feature. Then give users the right permissions, because access is controlled by permissions as well as by this page.

Your team heard that Business Central has Copilot, and someone asks whether it is on. The answer is more specific than yes or no. It depends on which capability, which permissions and, for some, where your data is processed. Five minutes on one page tells you the state of play. Here is what to check, based on Microsoft's documentation.

Who can configure Copilot?

You need SUPER permission, or one of several administrator licenses listed in Microsoft's documentation, such as an internal Business Central administrator or Dynamics 365 administrator. Business Central does not offer granular Copilot admin roles, so anyone with those rights can change these settings.

What are the admin steps?

Follow these steps:

  1. Search for Copilot & agent capabilities and open the page.
  2. If you see an Allow data movement toggle, decide whether to turn it on. With it off, capabilities that would process data across geographies are unavailable. The toggle disappears once an Azure OpenAI Service is available in your environment's geography.
  3. Review the list of capabilities, shown as active or inactive. Select a capability and choose Activate or Deactivate. Capabilities are active by default once they become available.
  4. Decide whether to enable Bing Search, an optional setting that lets Copilot search the web for some answers.
  5. Review the feedback settings, which are managed in the Power Platform admin center.

What should you check about data first?

Check where your data may be processed and who decides. The Allow data movement setting covers geographic processing for Business Central capabilities that use Microsoft-managed AI resources. It is not the same as Microsoft 365 Copilot tenant privacy or data residency, which are managed in the Microsoft 365 admin center.

For a Canadian business with residency expectations in contracts or regulation, read Microsoft's data movement article and ask your legal or compliance contact before you turn it on.

How do users get access?

Users need permissions as well. For example, a Copilot Sys Features permission set covers features such as Analysis assist, Autofill, Chat and Summarize. Other capabilities need page-level permissions. Agents have their own permission documentation and need additional setup.

Test with one pilot user before you widen access.

What should you test before wider use?

Test with non-sensitive data first. Check that the results are accurate, that users know to review suggestions, and that nothing posts without a person approving. Some changes take effect only after users sign out and in again.

Taylor’s Take

Copilot is a tool, not a policy. Decide what you want it to do, who may use it and what a person must always check. Turn on the capabilities that save time, and leave the rest until you understand them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I turn Copilot off completely?

Not as a whole. You can deactivate individual capabilities.

Is Copilot available in on-premises Business Central?

Microsoft's documentation says the setup described here applies to Business Central online.

Do I need to pay extra?

Check Microsoft's current licensing information and ask your partner. Terms can change.

Who reviews Copilot suggestions?

The user. Build the habit of checking before accepting.
