Why Your Accounts Receivable Balance Does Not Match the G/L in Business Central

Quick answer

Your accounts receivable total differs from the G/L when something posted to the receivables account without going through the customer ledger, when the two reports use different dates, or when currency adjustments post to one but not the other. Compare both totals at the same date, then check direct postings, report date basis and exchange rate adjustments, in that order.

The customer ledger says $412,000. The balance sheet says something else. Someone says "it's probably a timing thing," which is what people say when they have not looked yet. In Business Central the two views are built from related but different records, so a gap always has a cause. Three causes explain most gaps.

Why would two reports show different AR totals?

Because they read different data. The aged receivables report reads customer ledger entries. The balance sheet reads G/L entries. If a posting touches one and not the other, or if the reports use different dates, the totals drift apart.

Start by making the comparison fair: same company, same date, same currency view.

What are the three common causes?

Check them in this order:

  1. Direct postings to the receivables account. If a journal line posts straight to the G/L receivables account, the G/L moves and the customer ledger does not. Check whether the account allows direct posting, and look at the G/L entries for that account that have no customer link.
  2. Different dates. One report may use posting date and another due date or document date. Make sure both are as of the same date, and that nothing was posted after that date and changed one but not the other.
  3. Currency adjustments. Exchange rate adjustments can post to the customer ledger and the G/L at different moments, or with different results if one run was missed. Check when the last adjustment ran and whether it covered all currencies.

How do you trace a difference?

Work backward from the gap. Filter G/L entries on the receivables account for the date range. Compare them with customer ledger totals for the same range. Look for entries that appear in one list but not the other, or for amounts that differ.

Business Central also has reports that reconcile customer and vendor accounts to the G/L in many versions. Ask your administrator which one your version has.

How do you fix it safely?

Do not adjust balances to make them match. Find the entry that caused the gap and correct it with a proper journal, approved by your controller. Record the cause so it does not return.

Then close the door. Restrict direct posting on the receivables account, and add the reconciliation to your month-end checklist.

Taylor’s Take

A sub-ledger that matches the control account every month is a sign of a healthy system. A gap you have "always had" is usually a design problem. Fix it once, at the cause. (The ones that start with "it's only a few dollars" are the ones with the best stories.)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it normal for AR not to match the G/L?

No. With normal postings, they should agree at the same date. A gap needs an explanation.

Can I just post a journal to make them agree?

That hides the problem. Find the cause first, then correct it properly.

How often should I reconcile?

Every month, as part of close.

Does this apply to payables too?

Yes. The same checks work for vendor ledger and payables.
