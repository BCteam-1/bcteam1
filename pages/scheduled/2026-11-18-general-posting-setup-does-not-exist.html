“General Posting Setup Does Not Exist”: What It Means and How to Fix It

Quick answer

"General Posting Setup does not exist" means Business Central cannot find an account mapping for a pair of posting groups: a Gen. Business Posting Group from the customer or vendor and a Gen. Product Posting Group from the item or account. Read the pair named in the error, open the General Posting Setup page, add that combination and fill in the G/L accounts it needs.

You try to post an invoice and Business Central stops with "General Posting Setup does not exist," followed by two codes. It reads like a ticket from the system's lawyers. It is actually a simple message: no one has told Business Central which account to use for this combination of customer and product. Add the missing row and the invoice posts.

What does the error mean?

Business Central picks the G/L account for a sale or purchase by matching two posting groups. The business posting group comes from the customer or vendor. The product posting group comes from the item, resource or G/L account on the line.

The pair is looked up on the General Posting Setup page. If that pair has no row, you get the error. The message shows the two codes it could not find.

How do you fix it?

Add the missing combination and fill in the accounts. Follow these steps:

  1. Read the error and write down the two codes after "Gen. Bus. Posting Group" and "Gen. Prod. Posting Group".
  2. Search for General Posting Setup and open the page.
  3. Add a new line with that pair of codes. If a similar pair already exists, copy its account settings as a starting point.
  4. Fill in the accounts the transaction needs, such as the sales account for a sale or the purchase account for a purchase. Ask your accountant which accounts are right.
  5. Go back to the document and post again.

Why did it happen?

It usually happens after someone adds a new customer, vendor or item with a posting group that has not been used before. It also appears when a new product group is created and nobody adds it to the setup.

Treat it as a prompt to check the master data. Ask whether the new group was needed, or whether an existing group would do.

What about similar errors?

Other posting errors follow the same pattern with a different table. "VAT Posting Setup does not exist" refers to the tax groups. "Inventory Posting Setup does not exist" refers to the location and inventory group. Each one names the missing pair, and each fix is to add the row and its accounts.

A missing account on an existing row gives a different message, usually naming the field that must have a value. Fill in that field.

How do you stop it recurring?

Keep a short rule for creating new posting groups: who can add them, who updates the setup tables, and who checks the result. Run a posting preview on the first document that uses a new group.

A tidy chart of accounts helps too. Fewer groups means fewer rows to maintain.

Taylor’s Take

Posting groups are the wiring behind your numbers. Treat them as part of the foundation: designed once, changed carefully. If every new customer needs a fix, the design needs a review, not a patch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who should change General Posting Setup?

A finance lead or administrator who understands the accounts. Changes affect how every matching transaction posts, so restrict access.

Can I just copy another row?

Yes, as a starting point. Check every account on the copied row, since the wrong account will post silently.

Will the error come back for old documents?

Only if they use a pair that still has no row. Once the row exists, the same pair posts correctly.

Does the error mean my data is damaged?

No. Business Central stopped the posting before writing anything. Add the setup and post again.
