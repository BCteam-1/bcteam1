Preparing for Fiscal Year-End Tax Filing: What Business Central Reports Actually Support

Your accountant needs specific numbers in a specific format. Pulling them together shouldn't be a scramble if the right reports were set up in advance.

Your external accountant sends the year-end request list in early November, and half the items require pulling numbers Business Central technically has but nobody's built a clean report for — a breakdown that exists across three different screens, none of which export in the format the accountant actually needs. This is a predictable, avoidable scramble, and the fix is preparing the reporting structure well before filing season, not during it.

What Your Accountant Typically Needs

Beyond the standard trial balance and financial statements, year-end tax preparation usually requires a detailed fixed asset roll-forward showing additions, disposals, and depreciation for the year; a detailed general ledger showing all activity by account for the fiscal year; supporting schedules for any significant accruals or reserves; and depending on your situation, specific breakdowns tied to tax elections or credits your business is claiming.

Where This Connects to Setup Covered Elsewhere

Several pieces of your normal setup directly determine how painful this process is at year-end. A clean, properly maintained Chart of Accounts — covered in more depth in a related article on this site — means your trial balance actually maps sensibly to what your accountant needs, rather than requiring manual reclassification. Correctly configured Fixed Asset depreciation books, also covered elsewhere, mean your roll-forward schedule is accurate and available directly from the system rather than reconstructed by hand.

And custom Financial Reports, built with the specific column and row structure your accountant actually requests every year, mean this becomes a report you run rather than a document you build from scratch annually.

The Practical Fix: Build the Reports Once, Reuse Them Every Year

Rather than treating year-end reporting as an annual scramble, it's worth sitting down with your accountant once and identifying the exact reports and formats they need, then building those specifically as reusable Financial Reports or saved report configurations in Business Central. Once built, the following year's filing season becomes running an existing report against new-year data, not rebuilding the wheel under deadline pressure.

What to Prepare Before the Request List Arrives

Before your accountant's year-end request comes in, it's worth confirming directly: is your Chart of Accounts clean enough that your trial balance needs no manual reclassification? Is your fixed asset depreciation setup accurate and reconciled? Do you have a saved Financial Report configuration matching what your accountant actually asks for every year, or are you rebuilding that from scratch annually?

Where to Start

If this year's filing season felt like a scramble, the highest-value fix isn't working faster next time — it's sitting down with your accountant now, identifying exactly what they need in what format, and building that as a saved, reusable report structure before the next year-end request list arrives.
