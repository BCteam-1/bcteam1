How to Use Posting Preview in Business Central Before You Post

Quick answer

Posting preview shows what a document will create in the ledgers before you post it. Open the document, choose Preview Posting from the Posting menu, and check the G/L entries, the customer or vendor entries and the dimensions. If anything looks wrong, close the preview, fix the document or the setup, and post only when it is right.

An invoice is posted on Friday afternoon. On Monday the controller finds the revenue sits in the wrong account and the department dimension is blank. Fixing it means reversing entries and explaining the correction to the auditors. A thirty-second preview would have shown both problems. Business Central has this check built in, and most teams never use it.

What is posting preview in Business Central?

Posting preview is a read-only view of the entries a document would create if you posted it. Nothing is written to the ledgers. You see the result, then you decide.

It works on sales and purchase documents, journals and several other posting routines. You find it under the Posting actions on the document or journal.

How do you preview a posting?

Use these three steps on any document, before every post:

  1. Open the document, for example a sales invoice, and fill in all lines.
  2. Choose Preview Posting from the Posting menu. Business Central lists the entry types it would create, with a count for each.
  3. Open each entry type, starting with G/L Entries. Check the accounts, amounts, dates and dimensions. Close the preview when done.

Nothing was posted, so you can change the document and run the preview again.

What should you check in the preview?

Check four things: the account, the amount, the date and the dimensions. Most posting errors fall into one of those four.

  • Account: does the revenue or expense land in the account you expect for that item or service?
  • Amount: do totals, tax and discounts add up as the document shows?
  • Date: is the posting date in the right period?
  • Dimensions: are department, project or other required dimensions filled in on the G/L lines?

If the account is wrong, the cause is usually a posting group on the customer, vendor or item, not the document itself.

When does the preview show a setup problem?

If the preview fails with an error, the setup is missing something. A common case is a missing General Posting Setup combination, covered in our guide to that error on Nov 18.

If the preview works but the account is wrong, review the posting groups on the customer, vendor and item cards. Fix the master data, not just the document, or the same mistake will return on the next invoice.

Can you undo a wrong posting?

Some postings can be corrected with built-in correction functions, and others need reversing journal entries. Preview is better than either, because it avoids the correction altogether.

Ask your accountant which correction method fits your reporting needs, since it affects the audit trail.

Taylor’s Take

Make preview a habit for any new customer, new item or new type of transaction. Once a type of document has posted correctly a few times, you can relax. The first time is when the setup mistakes show.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does posting preview change any data?

No. It is read-only. Nothing is written to the ledgers until you post.

Can I preview a journal before posting?

Yes. General journals and other journals have a preview action under the Posting menu.

Why does the preview show an error?

The error usually points to missing setup, such as a posting group combination or a dimension rule. Fix the setup, then preview again.

Should every user preview every document?

Not every document. Focus on new transaction types, large amounts and any document where the accounts or dimensions are not obvious.
