QuickBooks to Business Central Migration Cost: What Is Inside the Number

Quick answer

A QuickBooks to Business Central migration cost has seven layers: licenses, implementation services, data migration, integrations, training, support after go-live and your own staff time. Published ranges differ because projects differ in scope, data quality and customization. The questions below show which choices move the price, so you can compare quotes on equal terms.

Three quotes arrive for the same move. One is a third of another. The cheapest one leaves out data migration, training and support. Comparing totals is a trap. Compare the layers. This guide lists each one and the question that exposes what a quote leaves out. We give no price here, since a number without your scope would only mislead.

What are the cost layers in a migration?

There are seven. Ask every vendor to quote or exclude each one in writing.

LayerWhat it coversQuestion to ask
LicensesMonthly subscription per userHow many of which license type, and from when?
ImplementationDesign, configuration, testingWhich modules and processes are included?
Data migrationCleaning and loading dataHow many years of history? Who cleans it?
IntegrationsLinks to other systemsWhich connections and who maintains them?
TrainingPreparing usersHow many sessions, for which roles?
SupportHelp after go-liveFor how long and at what rate?
Your staff timePeople on the projectHow many hours per week will we need to give?

Why do published ranges differ so much?

They differ because scope differs. A company with one entity, simple inventory and clean data costs less to move than one with several entities, custom reports and years of messy history. Vendors also price differently, and some quote only the first two layers.

Treat any figure you read online, including ours, as a reference range to test, not a quote.

Which choices move the price most?

Five choices drive cost the most: how much history you migrate, how many integrations you keep, how much customization you ask for, how clean your data is when you start, and whether you phase the project.

Cleaning your data first reduces the data migration line. See our post on preparing QuickBooks data.

How do you compare quotes?

Put each quote in a table with the seven layers as rows. Mark each cell as included, excluded or unclear. Ask the vendor to fill in the unclear ones. Only then compare totals.

Also ask what happens to the price when scope changes. A fixed price with a clear change process can be safer than a low estimate that grows.

What should you budget beyond the quote?

Budget for contingency and for your own staff time. Many plans forget the weeks when key people are busy testing instead of doing their regular jobs. Our ERP budget planning post goes into the lines most plans miss.

Taylor’s Take

A low quote is not a good price if it leaves out half the work. Ask what is excluded before you ask what is included. The right partner will happily show you the gaps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Business Central cost more than QuickBooks?

Usually, in subscription and project cost. The comparison that matters includes the staff time your workarounds consume today.

Can I phase the migration to spread the cost?

Often yes. Phasing helps cash flow and risk, but the foundation design should cover the whole business.

Is a fixed-price quote better?

It gives certainty if the scope is clear. It needs a good change process. See our fixed-price versus time-and-materials post.

What is the cheapest way to cut cost?

Clean your data before the project starts, and decide what history you really need.
