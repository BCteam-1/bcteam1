Business Central Keyboard Shortcuts and Search Tricks That Save Finance Teams Time

Quick answer

The most useful Business Central shortcuts for finance teams are Alt+Q to search, Alt+N for a new record, F9 to post, F5 to refresh, Shift+F3 to open the filter pane, Alt+Down Arrow to open a lookup, F8 to copy the field above and Alt+F2 to show or hide the FactBox. Microsoft lists them in its keyboard shortcut reference.

Every finance team has one person who moves through Business Central at twice the speed of everyone else. They are not working harder. They stopped reaching for the mouse. Eight shortcuts, each tied to a finance task, are enough to close most of that gap. These come from Microsoft's published list. Shortcuts can vary by browser and keyboard layout.

What shortcuts save the most time?

These eight cover the daily work of most finance users:

How does Tell Me search work best?

Type part of the page or action name. Tell Me searches pages, reports and actions, so you can open "Bank Account Reconciliations" without finding it in the menu. Learn the names of the five pages you use most and you will rarely need the menu.

How do filters help finance users?

Filters turn long ledgers into short answers. Use the filter pane on a ledger entry list to limit by date, account, customer or dimension. Save the combinations you use often, so month-end filters take one click.

What is the best way to learn them?

Learn one or two a week, starting with the actions you repeat most. Print a small card or share the list in your team channel. A shortcut you use daily sticks. One you read once does not.

Check that your own shortcuts work, since browser settings can capture some key combinations.

Taylor’s Take

Speed in an ERP is mostly habit. A team that knows search and filters spends less time hunting and more time checking numbers. Training should include these basics, not only the big processes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do shortcuts work the same on Mac?

Some differ. Microsoft's list shows the Mac equivalents for each shortcut.

Is there a shortcut for Preview Posting?

Microsoft's list does not document one that we could confirm. Use the Posting menu.

Can I change shortcuts?

Business Central does not let users remap them. Your browser may override some.

Where is the full list?

On Microsoft Learn, in the keyboard shortcuts article linked in this post.
