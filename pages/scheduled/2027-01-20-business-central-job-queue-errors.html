Failed Job Queue Entries in Business Central: How to Find and Fix Them

Quick answer

The job queue runs scheduled tasks in Business Central, such as posting in the background, sending emails and syncing data. When an entry fails, it stops running quietly. Open Job Queue Entries once a week, filter for entries in error status, read the error message, fix the cause and restart the entry. Ten minutes a week catches most failures.

Invoices are meant to post in the background overnight. For three weeks, they have not. Nobody was told, because the failure was a status on a page nobody opens. The job queue is the quiet engine room of Business Central. It works until it stops, and then it stops without a sound.

What is the job queue?

It is a list of tasks that Business Central runs on a schedule or in the background. Examples include background posting, email sending and certain data updates. Each task is a job queue entry with a status, a schedule and an owner.

Search for Job Queue Entries to see them.

How do you run a weekly check?

Use this ten-minute routine:

  1. Open Job Queue Entries.
  2. Filter the status column for entries in error, or sort by status.
  3. Open each failed entry and read the error message.
  4. Fix the cause. See the list below.
  5. Restart the entry, using the action on the page, and check that it runs.
  6. Note what failed and why.

What usually causes failures?

Most failures come from a short list:

  • Permissions. The user that runs the job lost access, or the account was disabled.
  • Setup changes. A required setting was changed or removed.
  • Data problems. A record the job needs is blocked or incomplete.
  • Dependencies. An extension update or integration changed behaviour.
  • Locked records. Two processes tried to use the same data at once.

The error message usually points to the area. Share it with your partner if it is unclear.

How do you stop silent failures?

Assign an owner for the job queue. Put the weekly check on a calendar. Review the job queue log for repeating errors, since a job that fails every night is a design problem, not bad luck.

Ask your partner whether you can set up alerts for failures in your version.

When is the job queue a performance issue?

Heavy jobs can compete with users for resources if they run in working hours. If users report slowness at the same time each day, look at what runs then. Move heavy jobs to quiet times.

Taylor’s Take

Anything that runs without a person watching needs a person who checks. Give the job queue an owner, a day and a calendar reminder. That small habit prevents most "why did this stop?" calls.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I delete failed entries?

You can, but find the cause first. Deleting hides the evidence.

Who should own the job queue?

The system administrator, with finance consulted on jobs that post.

Why does a job fail after an update?

Updates can change permissions, extensions or setup. Test jobs in a sandbox first.

How often does the queue run?

Each entry has its own schedule. Check the earliest and latest run times on the page.
