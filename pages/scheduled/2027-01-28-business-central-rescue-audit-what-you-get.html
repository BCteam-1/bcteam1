Business Central Rescue Audit: What You Get, What It Costs, How to Decide

Quick answer

A Business Central Rescue Audit is a fixed-fee diagnostic of an existing implementation. It finds root causes and gives you a prioritized fix plan. BC Team offers a Quick Audit, a targeted 5-day review, and a Full Deep-Dive Audit, a complete review over 10 business days. It is worth it when something is wrong and nobody can explain why.

Your reports do not match, month-end drags and the original partner has gone. You know something is wrong, but not what, and every quote you get is a guess. A diagnostic turns the guess into a plan. This post explains what BC Team's Rescue Audit includes and how to decide whether to buy one. Prices and scope come from our Rescue Audit page and may change, so check it for current details.

What is a Rescue Audit?

It is a structured review of your Business Central environment to find why it is not working as it should. It covers areas such as the chart of accounts, dimensions, posting groups, workflows, integrations, reports and user setup, and traces problems back to their causes.

What do you get?

Per our Rescue Audit page, you receive a written findings report, a root cause analysis, a prioritized fix plan with effort estimates and a live debrief. The Full Deep-Dive Audit also includes ROI estimates for each item, a week-by-week implementation roadmap and 30 days of post-audit support.

What are the two options?

When is it worth it?

It is worth it when you cannot say what is wrong, when quotes for fixes vary wildly, or when a previous partner left without documentation. It is less useful when you already know the cause and need someone to do the work.

How do you choose between the two?

Choose the Quick Audit when you roughly know where the problem lives. Choose the Full Deep-Dive when several problems compound, or when you need a full reset before spending more. If you are unsure, ask on the scoping call.

What happens after the audit?

You decide what to do with the plan. You can carry it out with your team, with BC Team or with another partner. The plan is yours.

Taylor’s Take

Do not pay for fixes before you pay for a diagnosis. A fixed-fee audit is cheap compared with six months of guessing. And if the audit says your system is fine, that is a result too.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, read access. The timeline starts after access and a scoping call.

Per our page, the audit is a one-time fee with no retainer. Check the page for current terms.

Yes. The report and plan are yours to use.

You get a clear report saying so, and a short list of housekeeping items.
