RAP in Practice: How AR Follow-Up Runs Week to Week

Quick answer

With RAP (Revenue Assurance Platform), installed inside Business Central, statements go out on a schedule with original invoices attached, follow-up escalates by age tier, collectors work from a daily priority queue, and disputes and promises to pay are logged in the same place. This post walks through a typical week using only features listed on the RAP page.

Most posts about AR software describe features. This one describes a Tuesday. What does a collector open, what do they do, and what happens without them? Everything below is drawn from what the RAP page lists, and no customer figures are claimed here. Your results will depend on your customers, terms and policy. For current features and plans, see the RAP page.

What does setup look like?

RAP installs from Microsoft AppSource into your existing Business Central environment. Per the RAP page, configuration covers customer groups, statement templates, escalation sequences and collection triggers, and BC Team's Taylor walks your team through it. After that, statements and follow-ups run on the rules you set.

What happens on Monday?

Statements go out on schedule. Per the RAP page, original invoices are attached automatically, so customers cannot say they never received them. The collections dashboard shows where each dollar stands by customer, aging bucket and collector.

What happens on Tuesday and Wednesday?

Follow-up escalates automatically by age. The RAP page describes tiered workflows at 30, 60 and 90+ days. Each collector opens their priority queue, which puts the highest-impact accounts first. They contact those customers and log the outcome.

This replaces the usual routine of sorting a spreadsheet to decide who to call.

What happens on Thursday?

Disputes and promises to pay get attention. Collectors log a dispute or a payment commitment inside Business Central, so the next person who opens the account sees the history. Promises that slip show up for follow-up.

What happens on Friday?

The finance lead reviews the dashboard and the week's promises. On the Growth plan, per the RAP page, a cash flow forecast built from the AR pipeline is also available. Look at what changed, and what you need to adjust in the rules.

What does RAP not do?

RAP runs your policy. It does not write one, and it is not built for teams outside Business Central or Dynamics NAV. The listed features do not include a customer payment portal. If your main problem is online payments, a different tool may suit you better.

Taylor’s Take

The point of automation is a calmer week, not a clever one. Same routine, every week, with fewer things falling through. That is what a good collections process feels like.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does RAP run inside Business Central?

Yes, per the RAP page it is installed as an extension from AppSource.

Who sets the escalation rules?

You do, with help from BC Team during setup.

Do I need a collections policy first?

Yes. See our collections policy template for a starting point.

How is RAP different from native reminders?

Native reminders are the base tool. RAP adds scheduling, tiers, a priority queue and dispute tracking. See the RAP page for details.
